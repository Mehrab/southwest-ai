    1 · Storyline only
    You are helping a volunteer coordinator.
Create a STORYLINE ONLY for a 6-slide presentation.
The audience is the community center board. They have 10 minutes and must decide whether to approve a pilot.
For each slide, write one headline: a full sentence that makes one point. Under it, list 2 or 3 short supporting points.
Tone: clear, plain and friendly.
Use only the facts in my notes. If something is missing or unclear, write [NEED: what is missing]. Do not add statistics or numbers that are not in my notes.
My notes:
[paste notes]

    Why storyline first: fixing six headlines takes a minute. Fixing six designed slides takes much longer.

    2 · Slides and speaker notes
    Now make the presentation from this storyline.
Keep the same number of slides. Use each headline as the slide title. No more than 3 short bullets per slide. No paragraphs on slides.
Write speaker notes for every slide: 3 or 4 sentences I would actually say, in plain spoken language. Put them in the speaker notes, not on the slide.
Do not add any number, date or name that is not in my notes. Keep every [NEED] tag.
Simple design, large text.
Create it as a PowerPoint file I can download.
    3 · Check the sources
    List every number, date and name in the slides and the speaker notes.
Next to each one, quote the line from my notes it came from.
If it did not come from my notes, say so.
    [NEED] is a to-do for you. Fill it in or cut the line before you present.

      After class: three more tools to try
      
  
      
    
      Gamma
      gamma.app. Paste your storyline and get a designed deck in about a minute.
      Free credits run out. Free decks carry a Gamma badge.
      
    
      Canva
      canva.com. Canva AI, then Design for me, then Presentation. Review the outline first.
      Free with limits. Exports to PowerPoint or PDF.
      
    
      NotebookLM
      notebooklm.google.com. Add your own sources, then Studio, then Slide Deck.
      Builds only from what you give it.

      Stuck?
      
  
      Claude didn't make a file
      Settings, then Capabilities: turn on file creation. Or ask: "Give me the slide titles, bullets and speaker notes as plain text." Paste them into Google Slides.
      
  
      Claude says I've hit a limit
      Switch to Gemini Canvas with the same storyline. Next time, fix the storyline in chat and ask for the file once, at the end.
      
  
      I can't find Canvas
      Look under Tools or the + in the prompt box. Still nothing? Make the deck in Claude, upload the file to Google Drive, and open it with Google Slides.
      
  
      The export has no speaker notes
      Use the "Speaker notes, if the export has none" prompt in Gemini Canvas and paste one block under each slide.
      
  
      Claude made the whole deck when I asked for a storyline
      Reply: "Storyline only, please: one headline sentence per slide with 2 or 3 points under it. No slides yet."
      
  
      The Copy button didn't work
      Press and hold the prompt text, choose Select all, then Copy.
      
  
      I'm on a phone
      Claude and Gemini both work in their phone apps. Finishing in Google Slides is easier on a laptop, so pair with a neighbor for that step.
